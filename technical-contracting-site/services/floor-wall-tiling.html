Floor & Wall Tiling for your property

Tiling can improve the durability, appearance, and ease of maintenance of a space. Planning the layout and preparing the substrate are important parts of achieving a neat result.

Practical tile installation for floors and walls with attention to alignment and finishing. The exact method, materials, schedule, and price depend on site conditions and the work agreed with the client.

What can the work involve?

Depending on the project, work may include substrate preparation, layout planning, tile fixing, grouting, and finishing around edges and fixtures.
